# Hi, I'm Keith 👋

I'm a digital marketer in Los Angeles. I get brands found on Google and in AI tools like ChatGPT, and I build the dashboards that keep a small marketing team on track.

**What I do**

- **SEO and AI search (GEO).** I find the questions people ask that nobody answers well, then answer them better. I also track how a brand shows up in ChatGPT, Claude and Google's AI answers.
- **Content.** Blogs, knowledge bases, newsletters and social carousels. I write like a human, and I check the numbers after.
- **Link building.** I pitch editors and write guest posts worth linking to.
- **Dashboards and AI workflows.** I build the tools that turn GA4, Search Console and content trackers into one page a team will actually open.

**A few results**

- Built a 99-page knowledge base from scratch that reached 148K+ search impressions
- Took LinkedIn engagement from 4.7% to 11.9% with two weekly carousel series
- Grew my site's Domain Rating from 5 to 10 with guest posts on 7 publications
- Brought in 67 leads at $37.24 each for a new landscape design company

**How I got here**

I started in mortgage and banking compliance, taught myself marketing, then picked up web development along the way. I've run Quinney Media Group, my freelance marketing shop, since 2021. Most recently I was a Marketing Associate at American Heritage Lending. I'm bilingual in English and Spanish.

**Open to remote roles in SEO, content and digital marketing.**
